Measure

Vanishing points give the camera. Where figures meet the floor gives depth. Bottles, tables and doors of known size give the scale.

Rebuild

Every figure and object is placed from its pixels in metres. Fade the original over the model to see where they agree.

Light

Switch to path tracing and the light bounces for real: soft shadows, and mirrors that show what they would actually show.

Each room labels what is measured, what is inferred and what is invented. Path tracing is gentle on a computer with a graphics card and slow without one. The paintings are in the public domain.
